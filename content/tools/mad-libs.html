Mad Libs Maker

How does this work?

Write a story and put your prompts inside square brackets: The [adjective] [animal] ate my [noun]. Anything in brackets becomes a blank to fill in.

To use the same answer in more than one blank, give it a name after a colon: [name:hero] anywhere else in the story will reuse whatever was typed for the first [name:hero].

Nothing is sent anywhere - the story lives in the page's URL, so "Copy link to share" gives you a link that opens straight to the blanks without spoiling the story. Your draft is also kept in this browser's local storage so you don't lose it on refresh.
