It's like the whole world is getting Boratted except everyone can see through it and his brazen refusal to admit that he's lying only increases the intrigue.

It's an unholy dynamic in the attention economy. Louis Theroux also studied it in his Manosphere documentary: the most shameless attention-maximizers can succeed because the algorithm lacks any ethical filter.
